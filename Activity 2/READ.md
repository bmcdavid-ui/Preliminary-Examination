# 🖌️ Activity 2: Color Palette and Typography

## 📌 Introduction

For **Activity 2: Color Palette and Typography**, I created a
**Color Palette & Typography Set** that represents a clean, simple,
and professional visual identity.

The purpose of this activity was to explore how colors and typography
can work together to create a consistent and recognizable design.
For my output, I chose a neutral color palette consisting of **Pure
Black, Deep Greige, White, and Soft White**. I also selected two
typefaces, **Marcellus** and **Montserrat**, to create a clear
distinction between headings and body text.

I wanted the overall design to have a minimalist and elegant
appearance while still maintaining readability and visual hierarchy.
The limited color palette helps keep the design organized, while the
combination of serif and sans-serif typography adds contrast and
balance.

## 🌈 Chosen Color Palette

I selected four neutral colors for my visual identity:

### ⚫ Pure Black — `#000000`

Pure Black is the dominant color in my palette, with a recommended
usage of approximately **75%**.

I selected black because it provides strong contrast and excellent
readability. It also gives the design a professional, modern, and
confident appearance.

As the primary color, black helps establish a strong visual foundation
for the rest of the palette.

---

### 🩶 Deep Greige — `#3D3B3A`

Deep Greige is used as an accent color and represents approximately
**5%** of the color usage.

I chose Deep Greige because it adds warmth and softness to the
otherwise black-and-white palette. It provides subtle contrast without
making the design feel too colorful or overwhelming.

This color also complements the minimalist and sophisticated style of
the overall visual identity.

---

### ⚪ White — `#FFFFFF`

White represents approximately **10%** of the color usage.

I selected white because it creates contrast against the darker colors
and provides visual space within the design.

White also helps maintain a clean and simple appearance while making
important elements easier to distinguish.

---

### 🤍 Soft White — `#F3F4F7`

Soft White represents approximately **10%** of the color usage.

I chose Soft White as an alternative to pure white because it creates a
slightly softer and more comfortable visual background.

It helps prevent the design from appearing too harsh while maintaining
the clean and minimalist aesthetic.

---

## 📊 Color Usage Guide

My recommended color distribution is:

| Color | Hex Code | Usage |
|---|---|---:|
| ⚫ Pure Black | `#000000` | 75% |
| 🤍 Soft White | `#F3F4F7` | 10% |
| ⚪ White | `#FFFFFF` | 10% |
| 🩶 Deep Greige | `#3D3B3A` | 5% |

The dominant use of black creates a strong identity, while the lighter
colors provide balance, spacing, and contrast. Deep Greige is used
sparingly as an accent so that it does not overpower the main palette.

# 🔤 Typography

For my typography system, I selected **Marcellus** and
**Montserrat**.

I chose these two fonts because they create a strong contrast between
elegant headings and clean, readable body text.

---

## ✨ Marcellus — Display Font

**Marcellus** is used for:

- Main titles
- Headings
- Large statements
- Important visual elements

I selected Marcellus because its serif style gives the design an
elegant, classic, and sophisticated appearance.

The distinctive letterforms help titles stand out while giving the
overall visual identity a more refined character.

---

## 🔠 Montserrat — Supporting Font

**Montserrat** is used for:

- Body text
- Descriptions
- Supporting information
- Secondary content

I selected Montserrat because it has a clean and modern sans-serif
style that is easy to read.

Its simplicity balances the more decorative appearance of Marcellus,
allowing longer information and descriptions to remain clear and
comfortable to read.

---

## ⚖️ Typography Combination

The combination of **Marcellus + Montserrat** creates contrast through
their different styles.

**Marcellus** provides elegance and personality, while **Montserrat**
provides simplicity and readability.

This creates a clear visual hierarchy:

**MARcellus → Main Titles and Headings**

**Montserrat → Body Text and Supporting Information**

Using two complementary typefaces also prevents the design from
becoming visually repetitive while maintaining consistency.

---

## 💡 Why I Selected These Colors and Typography

I selected this combination because I wanted my visual identity to
communicate a sense of **simplicity, professionalism, elegance, and
clarity**.

The neutral colors create a minimalist foundation, while the limited
use of Deep Greige adds warmth and subtle visual interest.

Marcellus gives the design a sophisticated personality, while
Montserrat ensures that supporting information remains readable.

Together, the color palette and typography create a visual system that
can be applied consistently across different designs, presentations,
and digital materials.

---

## 🛠️ Development Process

To create my Color Palette & Typography Set, I followed these steps:

1. Identified the visual style I wanted to achieve.
2. Selected a minimalist and neutral color direction.
3. Chose Pure Black as the dominant color.
4. Added White and Soft White for contrast and visual space.
5. Selected Deep Greige as a subtle accent color.
6. Tested different typography combinations.
7. Selected Marcellus for headings and major statements.
8. Selected Montserrat for body text and descriptions.
9. Established a recommended color usage guide.
10. Organized the final palette and typography into one visual system.

---

## 📚 What I Learned

Through this activity, I learned that color and typography are not
only decorative elements. They play an important role in communicating
personality, creating hierarchy, and improving readability.

I also learned that using a limited and consistent color palette can
make a design look more organized, while combining different
typography styles can create contrast and visual interest.

Overall, this activity helped me understand how carefully selected
colors and fonts can work together to create a consistent and
professional visual identity.
