## 📌 Introduction

For **Activity 1: Presentation Design Principles**, I created the following promotional digital poster as my output for the activity. The poster features a **"Free Coffee"** giveaway organized by the **Ateneo de Davao University School of Nursing**.

This output was created to practice and demonstrate my understanding of fundamental presentation and visual design principles. I aimed to create a poster that would immediately capture the audience's attention while presenting the important event information in a clear, organized, and visually appealing way.

In developing my output, I applied several design principles, including **visual hierarchy, contrast, focal point, movement, proximity, alignment, and balance**. These principles helped me organize the different elements of the poster and guide the viewer's attention toward the most important information.

Through this activity, I learned that effective presentation design is not only about creating an attractive visual output. It is also about making intentional design choices that improve communication, readability, audience engagement, and the overall visual experience.

## 📐 Design Principles Applied

For my Activity 1 output, I applied several presentation and visual
design principles to make the poster clear, attractive, and effective
in communicating the event information.

### 1. Visual Hierarchy

I used visual hierarchy to make sure that the most important
information would receive the most attention.

The words **"FREE COFFEE"** are placed prominently in the center of
the poster using large and distinctive typography. The supporting
message is placed at the top, while the date and location are placed
at the bottom.

This creates a clear order for the viewer to follow:

**Main Message → Supporting Information → Event Details**

This improved the presentation by allowing viewers to understand the
main purpose of the poster quickly without having to search through
the design for important information.

---

### 2. Contrast

I applied contrast through the difference between the light,
warm-toned background and the darker typography and coffee elements.

The black lettering of **"FREE COFFEE"** stands out against the
lighter background, while the brown coffee beans and caramel-colored
coffee splashes reinforce the coffee theme.

The use of contrast improves readability and helps important elements
stand out from the background.

---

### 3. Focal Point

The main focal point of the poster is the **"FREE COFFEE"** text in
the center.

I positioned the main message in the middle of the composition and
surrounded it with coffee beans and coffee splashes. This makes the
main message one of the first things the viewer notices.

Having a strong focal point improves the presentation because it
immediately communicates what the promotional poster is about.

---

### 4. Movement

I used the curved coffee splashes and floating coffee beans to create
a sense of movement throughout the poster.

The coffee elements direct the viewer's attention around the central
message rather than making the composition feel static.

This makes the poster more dynamic and visually engaging while also
supporting the coffee theme.

---

### 5. Balance

Balance was created by distributing the visual elements throughout
the composition.

The coffee beans and coffee splashes are positioned around the poster,
while the main title remains centered. The event details are placed
at the bottom-left and bottom-right areas.

This arrangement creates visual stability and prevents one side of the
poster from appearing excessively heavy.

---

### 6. Proximity and Organization

I grouped related information together according to its purpose.

The school logo and promotional message are positioned together near
the top, while the date and location are placed together near the
bottom.

This organization makes the information easier to scan and helps the
viewer understand the relationship between the different elements.

---

### 7. Consistency

I maintained a consistent coffee-inspired visual theme throughout the
poster.

The warm beige, brown, and caramel tones work together with the coffee
beans and liquid coffee graphics to create a unified appearance.

The consistent visual style makes the poster feel more cohesive and
professional.

---

## 💡 How the Design Principles Improved My Presentation

Applying these design principles helped transform my poster into a
clearer and more engaging visual presentation.

**Visual hierarchy** made the main message immediately noticeable,
while **contrast** improved the visibility of important text and
elements. The strong **focal point** helped communicate the purpose
of the poster quickly.

The use of **movement** through the coffee splashes and beans made the
design more dynamic and visually interesting. **Balance** helped
distribute the elements effectively, while **proximity and
organization** made the event information easier to understand.

Finally, **consistency** connected the colors, graphics, and typography
to the coffee theme, giving the poster a more unified appearance.

Overall, these principles helped me create a promotional poster that
is not only visually appealing but also functional. The design guides
the viewer's attention, communicates the important information clearly,
and creates an engaging visual experience.
