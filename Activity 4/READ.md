## 🌈 Overview

**Google Slides** is an online presentation tool that allows users to create, edit, share, and present slides while collaborating with others in real time.

> 💡 **Goal:** To create an organized, creative, and engaging presentation using Google Slides.

---

## 🎨 About Google Slides

Google Slides helps users create professional and visually appealing presentations using:

- 🖼️ Images and graphics
- 🎬 Videos
- 📊 Charts and tables
- ✨ Animations and transitions
- 👥 Real-time collaboration
- ☁️ Automatic saving
- 🔗 Easy sharing

It is especially useful for **school presentations, group projects, reports, and collaborative activities**.
